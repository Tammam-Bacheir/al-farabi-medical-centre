## 01 / Project Overview

**Al Farabi Medical Centre** is a healthcare website designed to introduce the medical centre, present its medical specialties, and make essential information easier for visitors to discover.

The experience brings together a **deep navy and turquoise visual identity**, structured content, Arabic and English presentation, and clearly positioned calls to action. The goal is simple: help visitors understand the centre's services and find their next step with confidence.

**Project focus**  
Healthcare communication · Service discovery · Clear navigation · Digital brand experience

---

## 02 / The Digital Experience

**01 — First Impression**  
A distinctive homepage introduces the centre through a strong visual identity, clear hierarchy, and prominent paths to services and appointment enquiries.

**02 — Medical Specialties**  
Organized specialty information supports visitors as they explore the centre's areas of medical care.

**03 — Patient Journey**  
A dedicated section presents the patient experience as an understandable, step-by-step narrative.

**04 — Frequently Asked Questions**  
An information-focused FAQ experience helps visitors navigate common questions and relevant topics.

**05 — Bilingual Presentation**  
Arabic and English content presentation supports a broader audience and gives the website a locally relevant experience.

---

## 03 / Design Direction

The website's presentation is built around a coherent healthcare-inspired visual language:

- **Deep navy** establishes a calm, sophisticated foundation.
- **Turquoise accents** create emphasis and strengthen the centre's identity.
- **Clear visual hierarchy** makes core information easier to scan.
- **Purposeful navigation** guides visitors toward relevant services and contact actions.
- **Consistent interface patterns** connect the site's sections into a unified experience.

> **Design principle:** Make medical information feel clear, approachable, and easy to explore.
